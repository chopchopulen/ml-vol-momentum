# FINAL_NUMBERS.md

Post-audit status of every headline number in this project.

Source of truth: `results/aligned_comparison.md` (regenerated by
`scripts/aligned_comparison.py` from cached forecasts — no model retrained, no
hyperparameter touched), `bench/BASELINE.md` (frozen pre-fix reference),
`audit/FINDINGS.md`, `audit/RECONCILIATION.md`.

Two panels, because "identical rows" has two defensible readings:

- **Panel A** — all 10 forecasters including the TCN, on their intersection:
  **123,749 rows · 2,236 dates · 56 tickers · 2003-02-12 → 2012-12-31.**
  The only sample on which the TCN's number can be compared to anything.
- **Panel B** — the 9 forecasters covering the full period, on their intersection:
  **274,632 rows · 4,897 dates · 61 tickers · 2003-02-12 → 2024-11-27.**
  The full-period comparison; the TCN is necessarily absent.

`*` marks a baseline added during the audit. All t-statistics are Newey-West at
lag 42 (= 2h) on the daily cross-sectional IC series; `N_eff` is the implied
number of independent dates under h = 21 overlapping targets.

## CLAIMABLE — with caveats

### C1. One architecture beats the zero-parameter baseline on the full period, by a small but significant margin

**Panel B, ΔIC vs a 126-day rolling variance:**

| model | XS-IC | ΔIC | t(NW) | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **transformer_fast** | **0.7455** | **+0.0085** | **+2.80** | **beats it** |
| lstm | 0.7382 | +0.0011 | +0.31 | tie |
| prob_lstm | 0.7372 | +0.0002 | +0.05 | tie |
| rolling_vol | 0.7370 | — | — | baseline |
| mlp_fast | 0.7217 | −0.0153 | −3.88 | worse |
| gbm | 0.6933 | −0.0437 | −12.96 | worse |
| ewma_094* | 0.6898 | −0.0473 | −15.25 | worse |
| har_rv | 0.6723 | −0.0647 | −15.02 | worse |
| garch | 0.6410 | −0.0960 | −12.90 | worse |
| const_floor* | 0.5505 | −0.1865 | −19.85 | worse |

**Caveats, all load-bearing:**
- **This corrects my own Phase 2 finding.** `audit/FINDINGS.md` S0-5 said "no model
  beats a 126-day rolling window." That was measured on the TCN's 2003–2012 sample.
  On the full period the Transformer does, at t = +2.80. The correction goes against
  my earlier conclusion and in the project's favour.
- The margin is **+0.0085**, against a measured seed σ of **0.0075**
  (`bench/BASELINE.md` §9b). It is roughly one standard deviation of seed noise.
- The Transformer was run at 2 seeds / 10 epochs / `d_model=16`; the LSTM at
  5 seeds / 50 epochs / `d_model=32` (`run_extension2_fast.py:43,47`). **The margin
  cannot be attributed to architecture** until both are run under one protocol with
  a seed distribution. See OPEN-1.
- It is one winner out of ~10 candidates. E[max of 10] under a seed-noise null is
  +0.0115 — larger than the margin itself.

**Claimable form:** *"On 274,632 identically-sampled rows, a Transformer forecaster
exceeds a zero-parameter 126-day rolling variance by ΔIC = +0.0085 (t = 2.80,
NW-42, N_eff ≈ 186). The margin is comparable to seed noise (σ = 0.0075) and to
the expected maximum over the 10 candidates evaluated, and the two models were not
run under a common protocol."*

### C2. Whatever the models are measuring, ~75% of it is static cross-sectional persistence

Panel B, same rows:

| model | XS-IC (levels) | XS-IC (changes) | XS-IC (ticker-demeaned) | pooled panel IC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| transformer_fast | 0.7455 | 0.5717 | 0.5066 | 0.7260 |
| lstm | 0.7382 | 0.5644 | 0.4960 | 0.7347 |
| rolling_vol | 0.7370 | 0.5568 | 0.4903 | 0.7111 |
| har_rv | 0.6723 | 0.4181 | 0.3857 | 0.6835 |
| garch | 0.6410 | 0.4958 | 0.3336 | 0.5092 |
| **const_floor*** | **0.5505** | 0.4185 | **0.1934** | 0.4523 |

A per-ticker constant, refreshed each fold with no time-varying information,
scores **0.5505**. Removing the ticker fixed effect drops every model to ~0.49–0.51
and the constant to 0.19.

**On the metric definitions** — a correction to the brief: the repo's headline IC was
**already** the per-date cross-sectional IC (`src/eval/tests.py:112`), not a pooled
panel figure. The pooled column above is computed only for contrast, and it is
*lower* than the cross-sectional figure, not higher. What is dominated by the ticker
fixed effect is the **cross-sectional IC itself** — because the cross-section of
equity volatility is mostly a permanent ordering. The right correction is the
ticker-demeaned column, which is now reported.

**Claimable form:** *"Cross-sectional IC on volatility levels runs 0.64–0.75 across
all models, against 0.55 for a zero-information per-ticker constant. After removing
the ticker fixed effect the models retain 0.49–0.51 against the constant's 0.19."*

### C3. Reproducibility: exact at fixed seed, σ = 0.0075 across seeds

Seed 42 run twice in separate processes returned `0.7302946209` both times
(max |Δforecast| = 0.0). Across 12 distinct seeds: mean 0.7296, **std 0.0075**,
min 0.7100, max 0.7421, range 0.0321.

Caveats: measured on windows 0–2 (not the full 10), single-seed, so the true
10-window σ is smaller. `TCNForecaster.fit` seeds `torch` and `numpy` but not
`random`, and does not set `torch.use_deterministic_algorithms(True)` —
determinism is observed, not enforced, and is not guaranteed on GPU.

**Passes the stated gate** (σ < 0.05). The headline was not a lucky seed. But σ
exceeds most of the differences this project asserts.

### C4. The Parkinson result is genuine — no leakage

`pk` at index *t* consumes bars **t−5 … t−1**; the target consumes **t+1 … t+21**.
Verified numerically to 1e-16, with a full one-bar dead zone. Standalone
cross-sectional IC: `pk` **0.6454** on 5 days vs `rv_m` **0.6552** on 21 days — a
range estimator is ~5× more efficient per bar, so 5 days of range carries about as
much information as 21 days of squared close-to-close returns.

**Caveat:** the 0.380 SHAP figure itself is computed **in-sample** (OPEN-6), is the
**GBM's** (not the TCN's), and is a share of mean|SHAP|, not variance explained. The
*feature* result is claimable; the *attribution number* is not.

### C5. There is real headroom in the sizing rule, and no model captures it

Panel B, identical rows, only the forecast fed to `vol_scale` varies:

| sizing input | Sharpe |
|---|---|
| **oracle — realized forward RV (perfect foresight)** | **+0.1120** |
| constant forecast (zero information) | +0.0281 |
| best real model (garch, and see OPEN-2) | +0.0861 |
| lstm | −0.0420 |
| momentum control, aligned | **−0.1343** |

Perfect foresight buys +0.084 Sharpe over a zero-information constant. **Caveat:**
these are not returns on capital — see OPEN-3. Use them only as relative brackets.

+2.00, −5.72) are the tell: with the loss zero on 99.98% of rows, whole groups of

**A correction to my own Phase 4 report:** I said "four conclusions invert." That
figure came from comparing against the *published* p-value matrix, which was itself
computed on unaligned rows — a comparison that conflates two defects and yields 3
significance flips, not 4. Holding rows fixed and fixing only the loss, which is the
correct measure of this bug, the answer is **10 of 10**. My earlier number was wrong.

## OPEN — known defects, deliberately not fixed in this pass

Scoped out as "minimal fix only". Each one currently limits what can be claimed.

| # | Open item | What it blocks |
|---|---|---|
| **OPEN-1** | **Protocol mismatch across architectures.** Transformer/MLP/TCN ran at 2 seeds / 10 epochs / `d_model=16`; LSTM at 5 seeds / 50 epochs / `d_model=32` (`run_extension2_fast.py:43,47`). No architecture has a seed distribution. | C1's +0.0085 cannot be attributed to architecture. No IC difference below ~0.015 in this project is interpretable. |
| **OPEN-2** | **GARCH is a per-ticker constant refreshed annually.** `baselines.py:179-194` broadcasts one scalar across each test year — 1 unique value per ticker-year vs 224 for every other model. | Every GARCH number, including its best-in-panel Sharpe (+0.086 Panel B, +0.312 Panel A). Pre-registered Prediction 1 ("HAR-RV beats GARCH") is untested. |
| **OPEN-3** | **Gross exposure is never normalised** (`portfolio.py:64-67`); mean gross 1.15 (rolling_vol) to 10.0 (garch, max 1810), floating 0.68→4.66 within one model. Plus a √21 annualisation error (`scaling.py:35`). | **No Sharpe in this project is a return on capital.** All Sharpe figures above are relative brackets only. Cross-model risk columns (max DD, ann vol) compare different leverages. |
| **OPEN-4** | **`rolling_vol`'s forecast level is 5.94× the target** (`baselines.py:26`: a 126-day sum against a 21-day target). | QLIKE and DM penalise it for the level error, not for skill — which is why R4's new matrix has it losing to everything. Spearman IC is blind to it. **Until this is fixed, no DM comparison involving `rolling_vol` is interpretable**, and R5's replacement verdict is unresolved: IC says tie (t = 0.31), QLIKE says LSTM wins, and QLIKE is contaminated. |
| **OPEN-5** | **Validation split is a ticker holdout, not chronological** (`lstm_model.py:59-85,129-133`), affecting LSTM/TCN/Transformer/MLP/prob-LSTM but not GBM. | Not a leak — all validation rows are ≤ train_end, behind the embargo, and `mse_resid_` is rank-preserving. But the four architectures being compared differ mainly in overfitting propensity and their overfitting control is inoperative. |
| **OPEN-6** | **SHAP computed in-sample** (`run_phase4.py:84-85` fits and attributes on `train_last`). | The 0.380 figure describes what the model fit, not what generalises. C4's *feature* claim stands; the number does not. |
| **OPEN-7** | **Universe is the alphabetically-first 80 tickers** at a fixed 2002 as-of date, with present-day sector labels and 19.9% empty sectors. | "S&P 500 cross-section" is not a supportable description. External validity generally. |
| **OPEN-8** | **65× annualised turnover** on a 12-month signal, from daily re-sizing against a 21-day horizon; `configs/default.yaml` specifies `rebalance: month_end` and the code does not implement it. Costs exceed gross alpha for every 22-window model. | Every net-of-cost Sharpe. |
| **OPEN-9** | **Tradable universe conditioned on the next 21 days** (`targets.py:19` + the inner join): names that halt over [t+1, t+21] are removed before sizing. Small, but genuine lookahead. | A methodology caveat on all strategy results. |
| **OPEN-10** | **`requirements.txt` does not resolve** on any Python on this machine (`torch==2.3.0` no 3.14 wheel; `arch==6.3.0` raises on import; `pandas==3.0.1` needs ≥3.11). | Independent reproduction. Audit env deviates as recorded in `bench/BASELINE.md` §0. |
| **OPEN-11** | **`max_drawdown` computed two incompatible ways** in two scripts that write the same summary file; `run_baselines.py` cannot run at all; forecast caches carry no provenance stamp. | Reported drawdowns; `make baselines`. |
